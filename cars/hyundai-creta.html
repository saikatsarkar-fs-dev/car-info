Hyundai Creta

The car that made the compact SUV the default family choice. The Creta's trick is breadth: three engines, four gearboxes and a variant list that stretches from sensible to nearly luxurious.

01 The short version

The Creta is less a single car than a family of them. The 1.5-litre petrol is the city-friendly base, the 1.5 diesel is for high-mileage highway users, and the 1.5 turbo is the one that makes the Creta feel quick. All three share the same roomy cabin and 433-litre boot.

The facelifted car sharpened the exterior and added a more screen-heavy cabin. Top variants get dual 10.25-inch displays, a panoramic sunroof, ventilated front seats and Level 2 ADAS. Lower variants are much more ordinary, so the variant you choose matters more than the badge.

Ride comfort is the Creta's strongest card. It absorbs broken roads without float, and the rear seat is one of the best in the class. It is not exciting to drive, but it is very easy to live with.

04 Safety and equipment

Six airbags, electronic stability control and tyre-pressure monitoring are part of the package. The top trims add Level 2 ADAS, a 360-degree camera, ventilated front seats, a panoramic sunroof and a Bose sound system. The Creta's feature list is one of the longest in the segment, but it is spread across a complicated variant tree.

05 The ownership notes

What works

  • Three engines cover city, highway and high-mileage use.
  • Rear-seat comfort and ride quality are class benchmarks.
  • 433-litre boot and 190 mm clearance make it a genuine family car.
  • The top variants are genuinely luxurious for the segment.

What to weigh up

  • Top variants are expensive for a compact SUV.
  • The turbo DCT is thirsty in stop-go traffic.
  • Base variants feel much less premium than the brochure car.
  • The steering is light and short on feedback.

Pick the powertrain first. The diesel is the long-distance choice, the turbo is the fun one, and the 1.5 petrol is the sensible one. Drive the variant you can actually afford, not the one in the brochure.
