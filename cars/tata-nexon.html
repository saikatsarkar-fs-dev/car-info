Tata Nexon

The subcompact SUV that made Tata a serious player. The Nexon combines a high-set cabin, a comfortable ride and a price that undercuts most rivals, but the engine and gearbox mix needs a careful read.

01 The short version

The Nexon is the reason Tata's showrooms stopped being quiet. It sits high, looks distinctive and packs a lot of equipment into a car that remains comfortably under four metres long. The facelifted version cleaned up the cabin and added a larger screen, but the fundamentals are unchanged.

There are three ways to power it: a 1.2-litre turbo petrol, a 1.5-litre diesel and a turbo CNG. The petrol is the value choice, the diesel is the highway workhorse, and the CNG is for high-mileage drivers who can live with a manual gearbox. The 7-speed DCA is the smoothest automatic of the bunch.

Ride comfort is a genuine strength. The Nexon feels bigger than its dimensions on a broken road, and the 208 mm of clearance means you rarely worry about what is under the bumper. The trade-off is a heavy, slightly wallowy feel when you push on.

04 Safety and equipment

Six airbags, electronic stability control and tyre-pressure monitoring are standard. Higher variants add a 360-degree camera, front parking sensors and a 10.25-inch touchscreen. The Nexon line has a strong crash-test reputation, but equipment varies sharply between the Smart base car and the top-end trims.

05 The ownership notes

What works

  • High seating position and 208 mm of clearance.
  • Comfortable ride on bad roads.
  • Petrol, diesel and CNG options under one roof.
  • Strong feature list for the money.

What to weigh up

  • The three-cylinder petrol sounds gruff under load.
  • The AMT is adequate rather than smooth.
  • Top variants get close to Creta money.
  • The heavy body blunts enthusiasm on a twisty road.

The CNG value play. The Nexon CNG makes sense for long commutes and high monthly mileage. You give up the automatic option and some boot space, but 100 PS and 170 Nm are enough for daily driving. Check local CNG availability before committing.
