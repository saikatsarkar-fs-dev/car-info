Before you write

A good correction is specific.

Car specifications change by variant, city and model year. A useful note tells me exactly which car and which number you are looking at.

If you are reporting a price, include the city and the date of the quote. If you are reporting a specification, include the variant and a link to the manufacturer page or brochure. Screenshots are helpful, but a source link is better.

  • The car and variant, for example “Creta 1.5 turbo DCT SX(O)”.
  • The figure that looks wrong, and what you believe it should be.
  • A manufacturer page, brochure or price list that supports the correction.
  • For prices: the city and the date the price was checked.
  • For images: the page and the image that failed to load.

Privacy. This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and has no server-side code. If you send email, your message lives in my inbox. If you open a GitHub issue, GitHub's own privacy policy applies. Image requests go to Wikimedia Commons and font requests go to Google Fonts, so those services see your IP address like any other web request.
